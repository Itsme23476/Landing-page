Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free trial work?

You pick a plan and start a 10-day free trial. We collect your card to start the trial, but you are not charged until day 10. Cancel anytime before then and you won't pay anything.

How much can I use voice?

Basic includes 90 minutes of talking a day, shared between dictation, voice search and the hands-free Assistant, plus 10,000 characters of spoken replies a day in a natural voice. Only your speech counts: silence and background noise are never sent, so most people never get close. If you do reach it, voice pauses until the next day and Filect tells you right away, and spoken replies switch to your computer's built-in voice, which has no limit. Pro is unlimited for normal use (fair use applies). Your allowance resets every day.

Do I need a Claude or ChatGPT subscription?

Only for the AI agents and the hands-free Assistant, which run on Claude Code (from Anthropic) or Codex (from OpenAI). They use your own Claude or ChatGPT plan, so Filect never charges you per task. If neither is installed yet, Filect sets it up in one click. Dictation, voice search, and file search and organizing work without one.

Can the agents do things without my permission?

Only if you let them. Choose Ask first and you approve each action before it happens, or choose Full access to let the agent work on its own. Either way you can follow every step live and see exactly what it did.

Does Filect work on Windows and Mac?

Yes. Every feature works on both: voice dictation, voice search, the hands-free Assistant, AI agents, and AI file search and organizing.

What happens after the 10-day trial?

Your plan begins and your card is charged for the first month. If you cancel before the trial ends, you're never billed and the app simply stops processing new files, nothing is deleted.

Do you offer refunds?

No. Every customer gets a full 10-day free trial to try Filect before paying, so all subscription payments are non-refundable, including partial periods, unused time, or change of mind. Cancel anytime before the trial ends and you're never charged.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no contracts. Cancel during the trial to avoid any charge, or later from inside the app, your subscription stays active until the end of the period you've paid for.

Can I switch plans?

Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade between Basic and Pro at any time from your account. For Enterprise, just reach out and we'll set it up with you.

What payment methods do you accept?

All major credit and debit cards, processed securely through Stripe. Filect never sees or stores your card details.

Is my data safe?

Yes. For voice, only your speech is sent, never the silence or background noise. File processing is handled securely via OpenAI's enterprise API. Your data is never stored by Filect and is never used to train public AI models. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.
