Cortana Replacement for Windows 11: Get Each Feature Back

November 24, 2026·7 min read

Quick answer

No single app replaces Cortana on Windows 11. Microsoft split its jobs up. Copilot takes the questions and the wake word ("Hey Copilot", off by default). Voice access takes opening apps, clicking and dictation. The Clock app still handles timers and alarms. If what you really miss is telling your PC to do something and having it done, a voice first assistant like Filect covers that, including your files, your calendar and your email.

If you used Cortana, you probably used it for a handful of small things: "Hey Cortana, set a timer for ten minutes", "remind me to call the dentist at 4", "open Spotify", "what is the weather tomorrow". Those little jobs are what people mean when they search for a Cortana replacement.

Microsoft retired Cortana as a standalone app in Windows in the spring of 2023, and the remaining versions in Teams mobile, Outlook mobile and Microsoft 365 mobile followed that fall. Get the wake word back with "Hey Copilot"

The closest thing to "Hey Cortana" is "Hey Copilot". It lives in the Microsoft Copilot app and Microsoft ships it switched off. To turn it on:

  1. Open the Microsoft Copilot app on your Windows desktop.
  2. Select Settings at the bottom of the left side menu.
  3. Select General.
  4. Turn on Listen for "Hey Copilot".

Three limits catch Cortana users off guard. It only works while the Copilot app is running, whether open, minimized or in the background. It will not start a new chat when the screen is locked or when another app is playing audio. And it needs an internet connection to answer; offline, the chat simply ends. The wake phrase is English only, although the conversation can continue in other supported languages.

Copilot is good at the question side of Cortana: weather, definitions, quick math, "how do I change my default browser". Where it differs is action. Copilot explains how to do things, and with Copilot Vision it can look at a window you share, but Microsoft says Vision will not click, type or scroll for you.

Open apps and control Windows with Voice access

Voice access is the replacement Microsoft names first on its Cortana end of support page. It is built into Windows 11, version 22H2 and later, it works offline, and it covers the "open this" side of Cortana well. You can say "open Settings", "click Save", "scroll down" or "switch to Edge", and dictate text into any field.

To turn it on, open Settings, go to Accessibility, then Speech, and switch on Voice access. The first time, Windows downloads the speech model and offers a short interactive guide that is worth the few minutes.

The catch is that Voice access follows commands literally, one step at a time. It does not understand a goal like "remind me about the dentist". Our full Voice access guide lists the commands that matter most and shows how to build custom voice shortcuts for the things you do every day.

Timers and alarms: the Clock app

This one is easy. Timers and alarms never belonged to Cortana; they live in the Windows Clock app, which is still there in Windows 11 with alarms, timers, a stopwatch and focus sessions. With Voice access running, say "open Clock", then click through to Timer and start one by voice. It works offline.

FAQ

Is Cortana still available on Windows 11?

No. Microsoft retired Cortana as a standalone app in Windows in the spring of 2023. Cortana in Teams mobile, Teams Rooms, Outlook mobile and Microsoft 365 mobile followed in the fall of 2023, and the Play My Emails feature in Outlook mobile ended in June 2024.

What does Microsoft recommend instead of Cortana?

Microsoft's end of support page points to three things: Voice access in Windows 11 for controlling your PC and writing text by voice, Microsoft Copilot for questions and help, and Copilot in Microsoft 365 apps for work with your calendar, email and documents.

Is there a wake word like "Hey Cortana" on Windows 11?

Yes. The Copilot app can listen for "Hey Copilot", but it is off by default. Turn it on in the Copilot app under Settings, then General. It only works while the app is running, needs the internet to answer, and does not start a chat when the screen is locked.

Is there a free Cortana replacement for Windows 11?

Voice access is built into Windows 11 at no extra cost and works offline, and the Copilot app's voice chat is included with Copilot. Filect is a paid app: Basic is $15 a month, and every plan starts with a 10-day free trial with a card required.
