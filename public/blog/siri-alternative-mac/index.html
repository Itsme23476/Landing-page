Siri Alternative for Mac: What Siri AI Does and What to Add

November 25, 2026·7 min read

Quick answer

Siri got a real upgrade on the Mac this fall: Siri AI in macOS 27 can use your messages and email for context, answer questions about what is on your screen, and talk to ChatGPT. But it is still a beta, it needs an M1 or newer Mac, and its server features have daily limits. The best Siri alternative depends on the gap you want to fill: Voice Control for hands free clicking and typing, the ChatGPT extension for open questions, or a voice first assistant like Filect if you want to talk to your Mac and have real work done in your files and apps.

People search for a Siri alternative on the Mac for different reasons. Some have an Intel Mac that the new Siri does not support. Some live where Siri AI is not offered. Most simply asked Siri to do something useful on their computer, got "here is what I found on the web", and gave up.

That last complaint is less fair than it was a year ago. Apple released Siri AI on September 14, 2026, built on models Apple says it made in collaboration with Google and its Gemini models. So before you replace Siri, it is worth knowing what it does now. This guide covers that first, then the gaps, then the alternatives. What Siri AI does on the Mac today

According to Apple's announcement and Mac support pages, Siri AI in macOS 27 can:

  • Use personal context. It can search your messages, email and photos. Apple's example: find a recipe a friend sent in Messages, pull the instructions from an email, and add the ingredients to a Reminders list.
  • Answer about your screen. With onscreen awareness it can answer questions or take actions about what you are looking at. On the Mac you can Control-click images, files or text to ask about them, or use a keyboard shortcut to select something on screen and type to Siri.
  • Search inside Spotlight. Siri AI is built into Spotlight on the Mac, so you can ask questions from the same box you use to open apps.
  • Hold a conversation. There is a new Siri app with your conversation history, synced through iCloud, so a chat started on your iPhone can continue on your Mac.
  • Write with you. Writing Tools let you draft or rewrite text almost anywhere you type.
  • Hand questions to ChatGPT. Turn on the ChatGPT extension and Siri can pass a request to ChatGPT, such as summarizing a PDF. You choose what is sent, and your Mac can ask you to confirm first.

On privacy, Apple says Siri AI runs on device and on its Private Cloud Compute servers, and that personal data handled there is not stored or made accessible to Apple. Requests you send through the ChatGPT extension go to ChatGPT instead.

Where Siri still falls short on a Mac

The limits come straight from Apple's own fine print, checked on November 25, 2026:

  • It is a beta. Siri AI began rolling out in beta, in English first, with more languages to follow.
  • It needs Apple silicon. Apple lists Mac models with M1 or later (plus MacBook Neo). Intel Macs are not on the list.
  • It is not everywhere. Siri AI is not available in China, not available to users under 13, and Apple says some features may not be available in all regions.
  • Server features are rationed. Siri AI's server side features have daily usage limits, and Apple says expanded access will be offered for a fee in the future.
  • Third party app actions are early. Siri works with apps whose developers support it, like texting in WhatsApp. Apple lists actions like drafting an email in Microsoft Outlook as coming "soon".

There is also a difference in kind. Siri is built to answer and to run quick, single actions. It does not hand a job to a coding agent that edits a project folder, runs commands and shows you each step. If that is the gap, you need a different kind of tool.

Voice Control: the built in alternative for hands free use

Voice Control is Apple's accessibility feature for running the whole Mac by voice. You say "click Save", "scroll down" or "open Safari", and you can dictate and edit text. To turn it on, open System Settings, click Accessibility, then Voice Control. The first time needs an internet connection for a one time download; after that, Apple says it works without one. The Voice Control pane also has an interactive tutorial worth the few minutes.

Voice Control works on Macs that Siri AI does not support, which makes it the natural pick for Intel Macs. The trade off is that it does exactly what you say, step by step. It will click a button for you; it will not understand "tidy up my Downloads". Our guide to controlling your computer with voice walks through it alongside the Windows equivalent.

The ChatGPT extension: a smarter answer engine inside Siri

If what bothered you was weak answers, the ChatGPT extension may be all you need. Once set up, you can say "ask ChatGPT to..." or let Siri decide when ChatGPT would help. Apple's examples include summarizing a PDF and suggesting recipes from a photo. It is still an answer engine, though. It explains and writes; it does not go into your folders and carry out a task.

Which Siri alternative should you pick?

  1. You have an Intel Mac or want everything offline: turn on Voice Control.
  2. You are on Siri AI and mainly want better answers: add the ChatGPT extension before installing anything.
  3. You want to say what you need and have it done in your files, folders and apps: try Filect alongside Siri. A sensible split is Siri for timers and messages, Filect for work.

You cannot uninstall Siri from macOS, but nothing stops you from running another assistant next to it. If you do not use Siri at all, you can turn it off in System Settings.

The bottom line

Siri AI made Siri a much better answer machine on the Mac, with personal context across Apple's own apps and awareness of your screen. It is still a beta with hardware, region and usage limits, and it is not built to take on multi step work in your files. Pick the alternative that fits the gap: Voice Control for hands free control, ChatGPT for answers, Filect for getting the work done.

FAQ

Is Siri AI available on Mac?

Yes, in beta. Apple began rolling out Siri AI in September 2026 in macOS 27, starting in English. It needs a Mac with M1 or later and a supported language, it is not available in China, and it is not available to users under 13.

Can I replace Siri on my Mac?

You cannot remove Siri from macOS, but you do not have to use it. You can turn it off in System Settings and run another assistant alongside or instead of it, such as Voice Control for hands free clicking and typing, the ChatGPT extension for open questions, or a voice first assistant like Filect for getting work done in your files and apps.

Does Siri work on Intel Macs?

Apple lists Siri AI and the new Apple Intelligence features for Mac models with M1 or later. Intel Macs are not on that list. Voice Control and classic Siri requests are separate features; check Apple's support pages for your exact macOS version.

Is there a free Siri alternative for Mac?

Voice Control is built into macOS at no extra cost and works offline after a one time download. Filect is a paid app with a 10-day free trial, and a card is required to start the trial.
