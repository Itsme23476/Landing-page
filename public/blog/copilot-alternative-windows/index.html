Copilot Alternative for Windows: 3 Options That Do More

November 26, 2026·8 min read

Quick answer

It depends on why you want to leave. For free, offline voice control of Windows, use the built in Voice access. For a chat assistant you can talk to, use the ChatGPT desktop app. For an assistant that hears what you want and does it on your PC, from finding a file to drafting an email, use a voice first assistant like Filect. To get rid of Copilot, uninstall it in Settings.

Microsoft Copilot is everywhere on Windows 11: in its own app, in Edge, in the Office apps, sometimes on its own keyboard key. People look for an alternative for three main reasons:

  • It explains instead of doing. You ask for help and get steps you still have to click through yourself.
  • You already prefer another AI. You pay for ChatGPT or Claude and do not want a second subscription.
  • You just want it out of the way. You never asked for it.

This guide covers all three: what Copilot can and cannot do today, three alternatives for different jobs, and how to switch Copilot off or remove it.

What Copilot on Windows actually does

The free Copilot app gives you a chat assistant with voice. You can talk to it, and it can listen for a wake phrase, "Hey Copilot", although that setting is off by default and only works while the app is running. It needs an internet connection to answer.

With Copilot Vision you can share a window or your desktop and ask about what is on it. Microsoft's support pages are clear about the limit: Vision can highlight parts of the screen to point you in the right direction, but it does not click, type or scroll for you. Vision also needs a Microsoft 365 Personal, Family or Premium subscription, or a work Copilot license. Microsoft 365 Premium, at $19.99 a month in the US at launch, replaced the standalone Copilot Pro plan in October 2025.

Microsoft is moving toward action. It has been previewing an agent feature called Copilot Actions for Windows Insiders. Its support page describes it as experimental and off by default, running in a separate agent workspace with access to a limited set of folders such as Documents and Downloads. Most people cannot rely on it yet.

Microsoft details here come from its own support pages and launch announcements, checked on November 26, 2026. Copilot features roll out by region and account, so confirm in the app before you rely on one.

Option 1: Voice access, the free built in alternative

If what you want from an assistant is "open Settings", "click Save", "scroll down" and typing without a keyboard, you may not need to install anything. Voice access is part of Windows 11, version 22H2 and later. It runs on your device, so it works without the internet and nothing you say goes to a chatbot.

To turn it on, open Settings, go to Accessibility, then Speech, and switch on Voice access. The first time, Windows downloads the speech model and offers a short guide worth the few minutes.

The tradeoff is that Voice access is not an AI. It follows commands literally, one step at a time, and does not understand a goal like "email last month's electricity bill to my accountant". Our full Voice access guide lists the commands that matter and shows how to set up custom voice shortcuts.

Pick it if you want hands free control of Windows at no cost, with no account and no cloud.

Option 2: The ChatGPT desktop app, if you already use ChatGPT

Often the real question is "can I use the AI I already like instead?". OpenAI's ChatGPT app for Windows includes voice, so you can talk an idea through or get something explained without opening a browser. OpenAI's help pages also say you can use voice in the desktop app to start tasks and check on their progress inside ChatGPT.

Its world is ChatGPT itself, though. OpenAI notes that Live voice does not support screen sharing, and it does not operate Windows for you. Voice allowances depend on your plan.

Pick it if conversation is the point, and you would rather pay for ChatGPT than for a Microsoft 365 plan.

How to turn Copilot off or uninstall it

If you want Copilot gone, you have two levels:

  1. Silence it. In the Copilot app, open Settings, then General, and make sure Listen for "Hey Copilot" is off. You can also right click its taskbar icon and unpin it.
  2. Uninstall it. Open Settings, go to Apps, then Installed apps, find Microsoft Copilot, select the three dots next to it and choose Uninstall. The Microsoft 365 Copilot app is a separate app with its own entry in the same list.

Copilot inside Edge and the Office apps is switched off in each app's own settings rather than uninstalled. On work PCs, your IT team may control this. You can reinstall the Copilot app from the Microsoft Store any time.
