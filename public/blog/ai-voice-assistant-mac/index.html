AI Voice Assistant for Mac: 4 Options Compared

November 27, 2026·7 min read

Quick answer

A Mac gives you four realistic AI voice assistants, and each is best at a different job. Siri AI in macOS 27 is the built in one for quick questions and your Apple apps, if your Mac has an M1 chip or later. Voice Control is the built in way to click and type by voice. The ChatGPT app for Mac is the most natural voice to think out loud with, and it can now look at the window in front of you. If you want to say a goal and have your Mac carry it out in your files and apps, that is what Filect is built for.

For years the Mac's voice assistant was Siri, and it mostly offered to search the web. That changed this fall. Apple shipped Siri AI in macOS 27, OpenAI gave its Mac app a much better voice mode, and a new kind of assistant can now take a spoken request and do the work on your computer.

So the real question is no longer "is there a good one?" but "which one fits what I want to say out loud?" This guide compares the four options by what their makers say they can do, with setup steps for each. If you came here because Siri itself let you down, our Siri alternative for Mac guide goes deeper on Siri's specific gaps.

The four jobs a Mac voice assistant can do

Decide which of these you care about first. No tool is the best at all four.

  • Talk. Ask a question out loud and hear the answer, with natural back and forth.
  • See. Look at what is on your screen, so "what does this error mean?" works without you describing it.
  • Type. Turn speech into text in whatever app you are using.
  • Act. Carry out the task: find the file, draft the email, sort the folder, edit the project.

Talk and See are now widely available on the Mac. Act is where the options split.

Option 1: Siri AI, the built in assistant

Siri AI is the biggest change to Siri on the Mac in years. According to Apple's Mac User Guide, you can ask about content on your screen, such as an image or a PDF, to get answers, search or take an action. Apple's own example is asking about a diagram open in Notes. It can also draw on personal context from your messages and email.

The requirements are strict, so check them before anything else:

  1. Your Mac must run macOS 27.
  2. It needs an M1 chip or later, or the MacBook Neo. Intel Macs are not supported.
  3. Your Siri language and device language must both be supported. The launch was in English first.
  4. It is not available in mainland China, and it is still labeled beta.

If your Mac qualifies, Apple's "Get started with Siri AI on Mac" guide walks you through turning it on. Pick it if your questions are about your own messages, mail and Apple apps, or you want fast answers without installing anything.

Option 2: Voice Control, the built in hands

Voice Control is Apple's accessibility feature for operating the whole Mac without a keyboard or mouse. It also works on Intel Macs, which Siri AI does not support, and after setup it does not need the internet. To turn it on:

  1. Open the Apple menu and choose System Settings.
  2. Click Accessibility in the sidebar. You may need to scroll down.
  3. Click Voice Control and turn it on.
  4. Stay online for the first run while your Mac downloads a one time file from Apple.

The trade off is that it does exactly what you say and nothing more. "Click Save" and "scroll down" work every time. "Tidy up my Downloads folder" does not, because it does not understand goals. Our guide to how to control your computer with voice covers the commands worth learning. Pick it if you need full hands free control, especially for accessibility.

Option 3: The ChatGPT app for Mac, for thinking out loud

The ChatGPT Mac app has one of the most natural voices to talk to, and it is good for working an idea through or getting something explained while your hands are busy. OpenAI's own walkthrough lists desktop voice for its Plus, Pro, Business, Edu and Enterprise plans.

It can now see, too. On a Mac you open the app's Settings, go to Voice, and turn on Screen context. Then say "take a look at this" during a voice chat and it captures the window in front of you to answer about it. macOS may ask you to grant Screen & System Audio Recording and Accessibility permissions first, and OpenAI suggests not sharing windows that show sensitive information.

What it gives you is an answer: an explanation, a draft, a next step. You still do the clicking. Pick it if conversation is the point and you already pay for ChatGPT.

Which one should you use?

  • "I have an Intel Mac." Siri AI is off the table, so start with Voice Control for hands free use.
  • "I want answers about my own messages and mail." Siri AI. Nothing else has that access to Apple's apps.
  • "I want to talk an idea through." The ChatGPT app, especially if you already pay for it.
  • "I cannot or do not want to use a mouse." Voice Control. It is built in and works offline.
  • "I want to say what I need and have it done." That is the Act job, and it is Filect's reason to exist.

These are not exclusive. A sensible setup is Siri for timers and messages, ChatGPT or Filect for questions about your screen, and Filect when you want the task finished. Switching between a Mac and a PC? The same comparison for the other side is in our guide to the AI voice assistant for Windows.

FAQ

What is the best AI voice assistant for Mac?

It depends on the job. Siri AI is best for quick questions and your Apple apps on Macs with M1 or later. Voice Control is best for running the Mac hands free with exact commands. The ChatGPT app is best for open conversation. Filect is best if you want to say a goal and have the work done in your files and apps.

Does Siri AI work on Intel Macs?

No. Apple lists Siri AI for Macs with an M1 chip or later, plus the MacBook Neo, running macOS 27 with a supported language. On an Intel Mac, Voice Control is the built in way to use your Mac by voice.

Can an AI voice assistant see my screen on a Mac?

Yes, several can. Siri AI has onscreen awareness for content like images and PDFs, and the ChatGPT Mac app can look at the frontmost window once you turn on Screen context in its Voice settings. Filect's hands free Assistant looks at your screen when you ask and can then act on what it sees.

Is there a free AI voice assistant for Mac?

Siri AI and Voice Control are included with macOS on supported Macs. Desktop voice in the ChatGPT app is listed for paid plans. Filect is a paid app with a 10-day free trial, and a card is required to start it.
